**Ridgeline Courier** — a 3D bush-plane courier game over a mountain archipelago. Fly the mail, thread the rings, land on short strips, and keep the old girl in one piece.

- **12 missions** with different goals: ring trials, mountain passes, night mail, island hopping, a rescue pickup, canyon slalom, heavy cargo, fire-watch water drops, and a 3-leg Grand Tour — plus Endless Rings and Free Flight.
- **4 airfields** (Ridgefield, Northport, East Bay, Coral Strip) with lit runways, windsocks, hangars and towers; **5 times of day** (dawn, day, dusk, night, storm) with wind, gusts and rain.
- **Real flight discipline**: throttle and turn management, stalls, overspeed damage, flaps and gear, fuel that runs out, hard-landing damage, and a damage model that ends the sortie if you push it too far.
- **Progression**: earn credits, buy 6 upgrades (bigger tank, better engine, reinforced gear, and more), unlock 8 feats, and keep a pilot profile with best times and scores.
- **Instruments**: airspeed, altitude, vertical speed, heading, artificial horizon, throttle/fuel/damage bars, a live minimap, and a mission checklist — with chase, cockpit and tower cameras.

Built with Three.js (vendored, no network), plain static files, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Fly with arrow keys / WASD (pitch and bank), Shift/Ctrl for throttle, Q/E rudder, F flaps, G gear, C camera, SPACE for the mission action, P pause, R restart.
